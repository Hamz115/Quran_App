Who Is QuranTrack For?

Whether you teach, memorize, or support someone's Quran journey — QuranTrack is made for you.

📚

Quran Teachers

Create classes, add students, assign daily Hifz, Sabqi, and Manzil portions. Mark mistakes at the word level and track each student's improvement over time.

📖

Students

See your assigned portions, view your mistake history, and read the Quran with beautiful page rendering. Know exactly where you need to improve.

👪

Parents

Stay informed about your child's progress. See which surahs they're working on, how many mistakes they made, and how they're improving week over week.

What Can QuranTrack Do?

Everything a teacher and student need — from class management to detailed progress reports.

📑

Class Management

Teachers create classes and add their students directly. Manage multiple classes, each with their own students and schedules.

📝

Daily Portions

Assign Hifz (new memorization), Sabqi (recent review), and Manzil (older review) for each class session. Select by page, surah, or juz.

🔎

Mistake Tracking

Tap on any word to mark a mistake. QuranTrack records exactly where each student struggles, so you can focus on what matters most.

📖

Beautiful Quran Reader

Read the Quran rendered exactly like the printed Madani Mushaf. Every page looks identical to the physical copy students use in class.

📈

Student Reports

View detailed reports showing performance trends, mistake patterns, and progress over time. Filter by month, surah, or juz.

📦

Export Reports

Export student reports as PDF, CSV, or Word documents. Share progress reports with parents or keep them for your records.

How It Works

Getting started is simple — here's what teachers and students do.

📚 For Teachers

1

Create an Account

Sign up and choose "Teacher" as your role.

2

Create a Class

Give your class a name and add your students to it.

3

Assign Portions

Select the pages or surahs for today's Hifz, Sabqi, and Manzil.

4

Track Mistakes

During recitation, tap any word the student gets wrong. It's recorded instantly.

5

Review & Report

View each student's progress, see repeated mistakes, and export reports.

📖 For Students

1

Create an Account

Sign up and choose "Student" as your role.

2

View Your Classes

Once your teacher adds you, the class appears in your dashboard automatically.

3

See Your Portions

View what you need to memorize and review for each session.

4

Read the Quran

Open the built-in Quran reader to practice your assigned pages.

5

Track Your Progress

See your mistakes, watch your improvement, and know where to focus.
